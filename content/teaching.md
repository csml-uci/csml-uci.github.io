## Truss Me!

[Truss Me!](/trussme/) is an educational structural simulation app created by Prof. Rimoli that teaches structural mechanics as a game: players design the lightest structure that can carry a given load and watch it deform, buckle, and fail in real time. The app has been downloaded more than one million times in 150 countries and is used to teach structural mechanics in high schools and leading universities around the world. A new version is coming soon, and it will run in the browser as well as on iOS and Android: see the [Truss Me! page](/trussme/).
