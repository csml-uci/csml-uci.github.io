## Abstract

The next stage of space exploration requires building infrastructure in space: large apertures and power systems in orbit, and antennas, towers, and shelters on the surface of the Moon. Everything that flies must first fit inside a launch fairing, so these systems have to be packed tightly for launch and deployed reliably on arrival, far from any crew and often with no possibility of repair. The lab works on novel deployable concepts for space infrastructure that are highly packable and mechanism-free, with no hinges, motors, or latches to add mass or to fail, so that a structure can be stowed compactly and unfold into a stiff, lightweight system once in place. Our interests span large structures for solar arrays and telescopes as well as lunar infrastructure such as deployable antennas and towers.
