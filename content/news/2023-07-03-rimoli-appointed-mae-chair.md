---
title: "Prof. Rimoli appointed chair of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering"
date: 2023-07-03
categories: ["people"]
---

Dean Magnus Egerstedt announced the appointment of Prof. Rimoli as chair of the UCI Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering for a three-year term that began on July 1, 2023. He succeeds Prof. Roger Rangel.

Read the full story: [UC Irvine Appoints Julián José Rimoli as Chair of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2023/7/uc-irvine-appoints-juli-n-jos-rimoli-chair-mechanical-and-aerospace-engineering) (UCI Samueli School of Engineering).
