---
title: "Lab joins the new Pratt & Whitney Center of Excellence for Solidification Science"
date: 2025-03-10
categories: ["research"]
---

UC Irvine and Pratt & Whitney have established a Center of Excellence for Solidification Science, directed by Distinguished Professor Diran Apelian, to advance the materials processing behind jet-engine components such as single-crystal turbine blades. Prof. Rimoli and Project Scientist Kevin Garanger are among the center's core faculty, alongside colleagues from the Department of Materials Science and Engineering.

Read the announcement: [UC Irvine and Pratt & Whitney establish materials science center of excellence](https://news.uci.edu/2025/03/10/uc-irvine-and-pratt-whitney-establish-materials-science-center-of-excellence/) (UCI News). Center website: [Advanced Casting Research Center](https://acrc.manufacturing.uci.edu/).
