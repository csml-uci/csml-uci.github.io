title: "Prof. Rimoli named ASME Fellow"
date: 2023-10-26

---

The American Society of Mechanical Engineers has elected Prof. Rimoli a Fellow, a distinction that recognizes significant engineering achievements. The announcement highlights his work on numerical methods for nonlinear microstructures and on metamaterials for energy absorption, together with his record as an educator, including the Truss Me! app that teaches structural mechanics as a game.

Read the full story: [Julián Rimoli Named ASME Fellow](https://engineering.uci.edu/news/2023/10/juli-n-rimoli-named-asme-fellow) (UCI Samueli School of Engineering).
